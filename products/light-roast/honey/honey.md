### 1. **Costa Rican** — $16.50

This coffee from Costa Rica offers a pleasant sweetness and balance. Notes of honey, caramel, and tropical fruits combine perfectly with a mild acidity, creating a harmonious cup with good body. A popular choice for those who enjoy sweet, well-rounded coffees.

### 2. **Brazilian Yellow Bourbon** — $14.90

A rich and sweet coffee from Brazil with notes of chocolate, nuts, and fruits. The honey processing enhances the smoothness and natural sweetness, making it an excellent option for espresso with deep, balanced flavor.

### 3. **Kenyan AA** — $18.20

Kenya is known for its vibrant acidity and complex flavors, but with honey processing, this coffee achieves a sweeter, more balanced profile. Expect notes of blackberries, honey, and light citrus acidity. A great choice for those seeking a balance between brightness and sweetness.

### 4. **Ethiopian Sidamo** — $17.00

This Ethiopian coffee is known for its complexity and fruitiness. Honey processing softens the cup, highlighting berry, floral, and honey notes without the sharp acidity typical of natural coffees. Perfect for filter and pour-over brewing.

---

### 5. **Guatemalan Antigua** — $15.80

From Guatemala, this coffee showcases rich honeyed and fruity notes like cherry and apple, with a light touch of chocolate. Its mild acidity and well-balanced sweetness make it a popular choice among those who prefer a smooth, yet vibrant coffee experience.
